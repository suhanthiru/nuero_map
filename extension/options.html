Neural Map: Gemini capture

Sends your finished Gemini turns to the Neural Map app on this computer (127.0.0.1 only).

Pairing

  1. In Neural Map, open Settings, Pair browser extension. Turn it on and copy the pairing string.
  2. Paste it below and choose Test connection.

Capture

Every finished turn on screen is sent, including older chats you open. Gemini doesn't show when a message was written, so older turns are saved with today's date.

When off, chats arrive without their Gemini title.
